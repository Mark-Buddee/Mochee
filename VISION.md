#Building Mochee-Pi

## Background
In 2021 I was formally introduced to the C programming language. Powerful and terse, I saw it as the right tool for every job. In that year I set out to write a program in C that could play chess better than myself.

Despite my best efforts at the board, Mochee beats me comfortably across all time controls. Mochee plays against bots online and can accept human challenges, beating friends of mine that I couldn't otherwise beat alone.

## Motivations
However, without a server host Mochee is hardly accessible to the public and progress is not continuous.

I want to make sharing my work and improving upon it much easier.

1. Easy for others
2. Easy for me
3. Push my (chess) understanding


, I can't share my work with others on the fly. I want a setup that allows others to play against my work at any time.
